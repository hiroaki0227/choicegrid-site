Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

ChoiceGrid (the “App”) is an Android app provided by Roomel Studio for creating and saving comparison tables on a device.

Information handled by the App

Comparison titles, option names, criteria, entered values, and saved URLs are stored in the App’s private storage on the user’s device. The App does not send this comparison data to servers operated by Roomel Studio, sell it to third parties, or use it for advertising.

The App does not provide account registration and does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Roomel Studio does not operate a server for the App.

Google Play Billing

The App uses Google Play Billing to offer and restore paid features. To do this, the App communicates with Google Play and receives product details and purchase records, including the product identifier, purchase state, and purchase token. This information is used only to determine access to paid features and acknowledge a purchase; the App does not send it to a server operated by Roomel Studio or store it persistently outside Google Play. Google may process payment and technical information under the Google Privacy Policy.

External URLs

If a user opens a URL saved in a comparison, the App transfers control to a browser or another external app. The privacy policy of the destination service applies after that point.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Comparison data remains on the device until the user deletes the comparison, clears the App’s storage, or uninstalls the App. ChoiceGrid is excluded from Android automatic backup. Deleted comparison data cannot be restored by Roomel Studio.

Google Play purchase history is associated with the user’s Google account and may be checked again through the App’s purchase restoration feature.

Children’s privacy

The App is not designed specifically for children and does not provide a feature for Roomel Studio to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. Material changes may also be announced through the store listing or in the App.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to roomel.studio@gmail.com.
